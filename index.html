About

Alan Peevers

I'm an electrical engineer who now works on climate and sustainability. I studied at MIT and UC Berkeley and spent years building embedded systems and software, so I'm comfortable going from a high-level strategy down to the circuit board and the code.

My focus is renewable and distributed energy, and the problem of balancing a grid that runs on more and more variable sources. I also serve on the board of a nonprofit that helps communities engage with climate action, and I co-founded Green City Mayor, a browser-based climate literacy game for middle and high school students.
